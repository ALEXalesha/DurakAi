**DurakAi 1.0.3: table labels are always visible.**

- With four players the fan of a side AI covered its label ("AI 2 · attacks …"), most of all with open cards (AI vs AI, "Show opponents' cards"). Side labels now sit above the fan, at the window edge, so the fan grows away from them.
- The face-down pile with a number at the right edge is the discard pile, but it looked like the hand of the AI that was already out. The piles now read "Deck N" and "Discard N" (Russian and English).
- In a large window the top player's label and yours went past the edge and were cut. They now get their own line sized to the font.
- With three players and open cards, the fans of the two top opponents overlapped and went off screen. Each now stays in its own half.
- New law test: player, deck and discard labels stay inside the table and never touch cards or each other; hands of different players never overlap. 9 table sizes up to a 4K screen, 2-4 players, hands up to 30 cards, plus real AI vs AI games in the window. On the old layout the first run (8 sizes) found 900 violations. Also: the line "AI 2 takes - you may throw in more" no longer runs into the next sentence. 11 new tests, 13 new mutants, all caught (104); 284 tests.

Files: `DurakAi-1.0.3-setup.exe` and `DurakAi-1.0.3-portable.zip`. The builds are not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn on first start.

Стопку бито с числом принимали за руку вышедшего ИИ - теперь под стопками «Колода N» и «Бито N». Строка «ИИ 2 берёт - можно подкинуть» больше не слипается со следующей. 13 новых мутантов, все пойманы (104); 284 проверки.
