**DurakAi 1.0.4: charts from the shared kit.**

- New chart "Win streaks and record": how many games in a row you won and your best streak (a draw or being the fool ends a streak).
- Statistics charts now come from the shared aikit 0.2.0 used by every game in the AI Game Room: two columns, one in a narrow window; labels that would not fit move to the next line instead of being cut; with no games yet each chart says in words what will appear.
- New tests: charts do not overlap, stubs with no games and with one game, labels stay whole in both themes, both languages, wide and narrow window; 3 new mutants, all caught; 320 checks.

Files: `DurakAi-1.0.4-setup.exe` and `DurakAi-1.0.4-portable.zip`. The builds are not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn on first start.
